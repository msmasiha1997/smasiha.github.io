Optimal Local Convergence Rates of Stochastic First-Order Methods under Local α-PL

Saeed Masiha, Saber Salehkaleybar, Niao He, Negar Kiyavash, Patrick Thiran

AISTATS · 2026

PDF Proceedings arXiv
TL;DR

Establishes matching bounds on the stochastic-gradient queries needed under a local α-Polyak–Łojasiewicz condition (1 ≤ α < 2), with a variance-reduced method attaining the rate. The guarantees assume initialization near a component of local minimizers and that iterates remain nearby with high probability.

Superquantile-Gibbs Relaxation for Minima-selection in Bilevel Optimization

Saeed Masiha, Zebang Shen, Negar Kiyavash, Niao He

Preprint · 2025

PDF arXiv
TL;DR

SQ-G tackles bilevel optimization with multiple lower-level minimizers by approximating the worst upper-level outcome through Langevin sampling and an upper-tail loss average. Under parameter-uniform local PL and additional assumptions, we establish approximation and optimization guarantees with explicit dependence on the minimizer manifold’s intrinsic dimension.
